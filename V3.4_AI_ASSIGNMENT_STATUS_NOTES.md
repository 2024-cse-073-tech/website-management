# FlowMate v3.4 — AI Assignment + Slack-style Status

## Project ownership
- Project-level manager defaults to the active workspace CEO and is persisted in `projects.project_manager_id`.
- Existing databases are migrated and existing projects are backfilled to the workspace CEO where possible.
- AI preview returns the CEO project manager for the review header.

## AI assignment
- AI still creates up to six main workstreams with detailed subtasks and nested child tasks.
- Main-task managers are diversified across suitable non-CEO members when the team allows it.
- Every direct subtask and nested child task is pre-assigned when at least one eligible member exists.
- Invalid/null AI owners are repaired server-side.
- Enough generated work is redistributed so every eligible member receives at least one execution assignment.
- On Leave, DND, and Travelling members are excluded from normal automatic assignment; Busy/Meeting/Focus remain lower preference.
- All assignments remain editable in the human review screen before commit.

## Slack-style work status
- Presets: Free, Busy, On Work, Work From Home, On Leave, Do Not Disturb, In a Meeting, Focus Time, Travelling, Custom.
- Optional status note and automatic clear time.
- Status appears in profile, avatar indicator, People directory, AI people hint, and AI manager/assignee dropdowns.
- AI planning reads current status from the database at preview time.
